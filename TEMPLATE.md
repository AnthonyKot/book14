# Book 14 — chapter template

The structure is a rhythm, not identical furniture. Every chapter must contain the
functions below, although headings and order after the choice may vary.

## 1. Cold open

One concrete, sourced disturbance near the decision point. No verdict and no outcome.

## 2. Role card

- **You are:** the historical role
- **Objective:** what success means now
- **Clock:** time remaining before commitment
- **Primary resource:** what is being spent to gain what
- **Authority:** what this role may decide
- **Constraint:** what this role cannot change alone

## 3. Evidence ledger

| Status | What belongs here |
|---|---|
| Known | Directly available at the time |
| Inferable | Supported by available evidence, but not directly observed |
| Possible | Plausible but unconfirmed explanation |
| Unknown | Information unavailable or absent from the surviving record |
| Unknowable | Private reasoning or communication no source establishes |

## 4. Decision point

State two or three plausible options with their expected gain, exposed risk and trigger
for reconsideration. Then stop:

> What do you do? What fact would make you change your answer?

## 5. Outcome

Reveal what happened in the minimum chronology needed. Outcome is evidence about the
world, not proof that the preceding decision was bad.

## 6. The people who inherited it

Shift roles. Give the downstream actor their own state, information and options. Ask what
they could still preserve, communicate, refuse, rescue or improvise.

## 7. Reconstruction

Locate the earliest reversible and consequential decision. Separate:

- decision quality;
- execution quality;
- outcome quality.

## 8. Competing diagnoses

At least two of: decision failure, execution failure, communication failure, insufficient
information, reasonable risk/bad outcome, distributed causes.

## 9. Counterfactual

Change one variable. State assumptions. Explain whether it changes the later problem in
kind or degree.

## 10. Judgement

Give the best-supported diagnosis and confidence level. State what the record cannot
establish.

## 11. Where this stops being true

Show the condition under which the recommendation reverses, or a case where accepting
the risk is justified.

## 12. Transfer

- **What transfers**
- **What does not transfer**
- **Solo-actionable version**

## 13. Field exercise

- observable moment;
- one question;
- recording method;
- pass condition;
- review condition;
- classification: decision / execution / uncertain.

## 14. Receipts

Primary records first, then official investigation, participant accounts, expert
analysis and retrospective interpretation. Record access and reuse rights separately.

## Closing line

Working refrain:

> Before somebody has to be brave, where is the last reversible decision?
