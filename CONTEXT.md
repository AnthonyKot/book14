# Book 14 — CONTEXT

Working title: **Historical Fuck-Ups**

Working subtitle: **The decision was made upstairs. The danger arrived downstairs.**

This is the authority document. Precedence: this file → `AGENT.md` → the chapter
contract in `dossiers/` → `TEMPLATE.md` → prose.

## Core promise

This is not a catalogue of disasters and it is not a collection of clever verdicts.
Each chapter reconstructs one real choice before the result was known, asks the reader
to commit, and then follows the people who inherited the risk.

The intended change is:

> I notice when a plan has lost one of the assumptions that made it plausible, and I
> know what to ask before somebody else is required to become brave.

## Thesis

Organizations often remember the people who rescued a bad situation more vividly than
the people who made the situation likely. Courage is real. It can also become the final
stage of risk transfer: an optimistic promise is made by one group, operational danger
is inherited by another, and exceptional action later turns the whole event into a hero
story.

The book must test that thesis, not force every case to prove it. Permitted findings are:

- poor decision, competent execution, bad outcome;
- reasonable decision, poor execution;
- reasonable risk, bad outcome;
- distributed failure with no single decisive error;
- evidence insufficient.

## Reader

An intelligent adult who knows the famous ending but has never been asked to decide
before seeing it. They do not need another school history. They need a playable case,
a disciplined evidence horizon, and a diagnostic method that transfers to ordinary
work.

## The eight-case pilot

1. Challenger
2. BP Texas City refinery
3. Boeing 737 MAX
4. Titanic
5. Bay of Pigs
6. Triangle Shirtwaist Factory
7. Scott versus Amundsen
8. Iraq, 2003 — invasion and post-conflict planning

These are pilots, not a final table of contents. Drafting exists to discover which cases
deserve the book.

## The recurring mechanism

Each case follows this transfer:

`promise → warning → commitment → inherited danger → improvisation → public memory`

The sequence is a diagnostic lens, not a mandatory plot. A chapter must say where it
does not fit.

## Non-negotiable evidence rules

1. Preserve the decision horizon. No fact learned later enters the choice screen.
2. Separate **known**, **reasonably inferable**, **possible**, **unknown**, and
   **unknowable**.
3. Separate observation, contemporary interpretation, author inference, and unresolved
   hypothesis.
4. Every causal claim needs a counterfactual and at least one competing diagnosis.
5. An earlier event is not automatically a cause. Find the earliest reversible and
   consequential decision.
6. Do not infer private intention from outcome. “Wanted,” “knew,” “believed,” and
   “ignored” require evidence.
7. Primary and official records outrank retrospective narrative. Free access does not
   imply permission to reproduce.
8. Every chapter carries downloadable receipts sufficient for a reader to challenge it.

## Voice

Calm, exact, vivid and unsentimental. The reader is challenged, never humiliated.
Profanity belongs to the title and occasional earned emphasis, not as a substitute for
analysis. No omniscient narration. No motivational filler. No generic “leadership
lessons.”

Avoid hindsight phrases: “obviously,” “already over,” “had no choice,” “should have
known,” “could never work.” Prefer bounded claims: “removed one option,” “made failure
more likely,” “the record supports,” “the evidence cannot establish.”

## Human rule

The person inheriting the danger is not a prop used to punish management. Give them an
objective, incomplete information, constraints and agency of their own. Heroism must be
described as action, not used to redeem the system that required it.

## Transfer rule

Every chapter ends with one decision the reader can make without controlling an entire
organization. The exercise must include an observable moment, one question, a recording
method, a pass condition and a review condition.

## Definition of pilot-ready

A dossier is ready for prose only when:

- the decision point is visible;
- at least two options were genuinely available;
- the evidence horizon can be reconstructed;
- an identifiable person or group inherits the consequence;
- the preferred diagnosis survives a counterfactual;
- a credible competing diagnosis remains in the chapter;
- the boundary is explicit;
- the principal sources are free to access and locally archivable;
- reuse rights are recorded separately from access rights.
